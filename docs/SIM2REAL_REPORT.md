# ARGUS simulation-to-reality report

## Outcome

ARGUS now has a reproducible, measured-data bridge rather than a simulator-only training path. It combines three distinct forms of evidence:

1. KU Leuven LMSD supplies real CFRP multistatic frequency responses and known added-mass locations.
2. TU Darmstadt supplies microphone taps from eight physical GFRP plates with intact, delamination, and impact-damage labels.
3. The University of Bologna record supplies paired fourth-order ray-tracing simulations and measured aluminium impact signals with known coordinates.

These sources reduce different parts of the gap. They do not make the unsupported claim that public data validates ARGUS on the user's eventual laptop/mobile apparatus.

## Reproduce everything

```powershell
python scripts\download_lmsd_dataset.py --profile all
python scripts\prepare_lmsd_dataset.py

python scripts\download_sim2real_datasets.py
python scripts\prepare_tud_gfrp_dataset.py
python scripts\fit_sim2real_models.py

python scripts\prepare_ae_impact_dataset.py
python scripts\benchmark_ae_sim2real.py

python -m pytest backend\tests -q
```

All external files are accepted only after size and publisher-declared MD5 verification. Every local external-data directory contains an `argus_manifest.json` with the record, license, selected files, and checksums. Archives are rejected if a member would escape the declared extraction directory.

## Measured GFRP microphone bridge

Source: [TU Darmstadt record 33756af4-eb6d-4156-8708-a41cbed33e7b](https://tudatalib.ulb.tu-darmstadt.de/items/33756af4-eb6d-4156-8708-a41cbed33e7b), CC BY 4.0.

The selected unaugmented archive contains 2,568 0.1-second float WAV signals sampled at 51.2 kHz:

- 2,100 intact point measurements;
- 468 defect point measurements;
- eight independent 360 x 280 mm GFRP plates;
- a 48-point spatial grid, two sides, and repeated tapping runs;
- delamination specimens and impact-damaged specimens.

`prepare_tud_gfrp_dataset.py` converts every waveform to mono, resamples it by a rational polyphase filter to 16 kHz, and applies the production ARGUS 250-7,000 Hz feature contract. It preserves physical plate, side, run, point, normalized coordinates, label, source sample rate, relative path, record, and license.

### Leakage-resistant result

The real-data detector uses only gain-robust acoustic features. Four-fold `GroupKFold` holds out complete physical plates; repeated taps, plate sides, and spatial points from a held-out plate cannot leak into training.

| Metric | Complete-plate-held-out result |
|---|---:|
| Balanced accuracy | 0.775 |
| ROC-AUC | 0.911 |
| Average precision | 0.699 |
| F1 | 0.634 |
| Brier score | 0.097 |
| Confusion matrix | TN 1,932; FP 168; FN 173; TP 295 |

This demonstrates useful real acoustic defect information on unseen specimens. It does not establish arbitrary 2D localization because each example is a pointwise tap/measurement rather than an ARGUS multistatic source-receiver experiment.

### Feature transport and runtime guard

`fit_sim2real_models.py` fits separate healthy and damage robust CORAL transports. The transforms align simulator and measured first/second feature moments while preserving the source and target feature contracts. This avoids treating amplitudes and spectral statistics generated by the simulator as if they were already microphone-calibrated.

Alignment diagnostics on the fitted datasets were:

| State | Standardized Wasserstein before | After | Relative covariance error before | After |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Healthy | 97.964 | 0.194 | 2,086,762.055 | 0.062 |
| Damage | 0.261 | 0.201 | 17.097 | 0.100 |

The extreme healthy pre-alignment value exposes severe simulator/microphone mismatch in at least one low-variance feature; it is not itself a performance score. The post-alignment values are descriptive in-sample moment matching, not evidence of unseen-device generalization.

The runtime loads `models/sim2real_acoustic_reference.json`. When a waveform comes from a WAV upload or physical device, its gain-robust feature distance is ranked against the 2,568 measured signals. The central 90% of the empirical reference adds no penalty. Only the upper tail contributes to the existing OOD caution/abstention decision. In-process simulation measurements do not use this physical-reference score.

## Paired impact-localization bridge

Source: [Zenodo record 10875042](https://zenodo.org/records/10875042), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.10875042`, CC BY 4.0.

The adapter processes:

- 1,000 simulated Tx-Rx paths: 40 transmitter coordinates x 25 receiver coordinates;
- ray-traced boundary reflections through fourth order;
- publisher-provided time-of-arrival labels;
- nine measured impact positions and three sensor channels on a 1 x 1 x 0.003 m aluminium plate;
- a shared 2 MHz sampling rate and 250 kHz excitation domain.

The simulated geometric fit estimates an effective velocity of about 3,044 m/s with approximately 2.60 microseconds geometric residual. A waveform-based ToA regressor evaluated with complete transmitter groups held out obtained 22.18 microseconds MAE and R2 0.599.

For the measured positions, the simulation-transferred physics localizer produced 0.175 m mean error. A leave-one-position-out few-shot localizer trained on the other eight measured positions produced 0.141 m mean error, a 19.6% reduction. An affine timing correction increased error to 0.197 m, so the negative-transfer guard marks that correction rejected instead of deploying a worse calibration.

This result demonstrates why a small amount of physical calibration matters. Its scope is one aluminium plate, three sensors, nine impact positions, and a 0.4-0.7 m interior grid. It is not a claim about cavities, delaminations, phone microphones, other materials, plate edges, or field conditions.

## What is genuinely closed

- Public real waveforms are downloaded, checksum-verified, attributed, and adapted reproducibly.
- Physical plate identity is enforced in evaluation rather than randomly splitting correlated repeats.
- A measured acoustic reference now limits confidence on external physical signals outside the observed domain.
- Simulator feature moments can be transported separately for healthy and damaged hypotheses.
- A small measured calibration set demonstrates a 19.6% impact-localization improvement over direct simulation transfer.
- Harmful calibration is detected and rejected using held-out physical positions.

## What remains open

- No public dataset exactly reproduces the user's two-laptop/one-phone clocks, microphone responses, speaker excitation, mounting, room, panel, or frequency program.
- The real GFRP result is pointwise defect detection, not adaptive multistatic localization.
- The real impact result contains one plate and transient impacts, not persistent hidden defects.
- The current evidence cannot support field certification, a guaranteed localization tolerance, or market-leader superiority.

The minimum remaining closure experiment is a healthy panel plus approximately ten reversible-defect locations recorded with the actual laptops/mobile. Keep entire locations or specimens held out, capture healthy references before and after each block, and record every candidate experiment needed for offline policy replay. The exact acquisition table and split policy are in `docs/REAL_DATA_GUIDE.md`.
