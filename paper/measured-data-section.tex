\section{Measured-Source Assessments: 8--9 October 2026}
\label{sec:measured-evidence}

Four separately scoped studies use published measured recordings to test
screening, source localization, sequential path replay, and signal quality.
Table~\ref{tab:measured-units} identifies their distinct evaluation units.
Protocols fix grouping, preprocessing, models, and thresholds before the new
fits; complete predictions, poor cases, and source/input hashes are retained in
the four dated measured-study JSON reports. GFRP and Bologna are reanalyses of
previously examined records, rather than independently acquired blind trials.
The new OGW and MORPHO analyses use separate ultrasonic instruments. These
studies have not exercised or validated the ARGUS physical apparatus.

\subsection{Whole-Plate Audio Screening}
The TU Darmstadt GFRP record~\cite{feldmann2026dataset} supplies 2,568
unaugmented microphone taps from eight plates: 2,100 intact-labelled and 468
defect-labelled recordings. Each clip contains 5,120 samples at 51.2 kHz.
The new study verifies publisher labels, original archive/member integrity,
and decoded-recording duplicates, then recomputes the 15 fixed ARGUS features
after the declared per-record resampling/filtering. Filenames, coordinates,
plate identities, and labels do not enter the feature matrix. Four grouped
folds keep complete plates apart; standardization uses training rows only.
No previously fitted CORAL map, acoustic reference, or model checkpoint is used.

Fixed logistic regression yields pooled out-of-fold balanced accuracy 0.8381
and AUROC 0.9222, versus 0.5000 for the no-feature majority baseline.
A 2,000-resample bootstrap of the eight complete plate clusters gives a
balanced-accuracy interval [0.7521, 0.9154]. This interval conditions on the
already fitted out-of-fold predictions, without model refitting. All 2,568
records receive predictions. Performance is heterogeneous: plate 5 has 188
false positives among 240 intact-labelled taps, whereas several other plates
perform substantially better. The result supports dataset-conditioned audio
screening, with eight specimen groups; it establishes neither a defect
coordinate nor adaptive guided-wave inspection or field probability of detection.

\subsection{Previously Seen Transient-Source Localization}
The Bologna dataset~\cite{zonzini2024dataset} contributes nine measured source
positions with three simultaneous channels per position on one aluminum plate.
Its native rate is 2 MHz. Each fold holds out a complete position and fits
timing or feature calibration on the other eight; channels from one event
remain together. The fixed 5\% cumulative-energy time picker feeds an isotropic
time-difference-of-arrival model with training-fitted effective speed and
relative sensor offsets.

Mean source error is 108.99 mm for calibrated TDoA, compared with 181.07 mm for
the training-position centroid and 140.56 mm for three-nearest-neighbor
regression. Only one of nine TDoA predictions lies within 50 mm. No independent
repeat axis or second specimen is available. These descriptive improvements
concern a transient source on a previously seen public record, not the
location of a persistent hidden defect; no specimen-generalization interval
is inferred from the nine positions.

\subsection{Native Ultrasonic Offline Path Replay}
The OGW temperature extension~\cite{moll2019temperature,moll2019temperaturedata}
provides twelve transducers on a 500 by 500 mm CFRP plate. A protocol-index
subset contains 18 complete banks at nominal 20, 40, and 60~$^{\circ}$C, with
40 kHz excitation and native 10 MHz sampling. Each bank has 66 actually
recorded unordered source--receiver pairs; reciprocal observations are not
invented. Four reference locations use removable surface-mounted masses.
Original ZIP-member lengths, CRC32, and local SHA-256 are verified; the
whole-archive publisher MD5 remains unverified because only bounded members
were downloaded.

Healthy thermal cycle 1 supplies reference waveforms; cycle 2 supplies reserved
healthy tests in this dated replay. A position and all its temperatures are held out together.
The scalar observation is $1-|\rho|$ between reference and catch waveforms.
A fixed RAPID ellipse kernel, with $\beta=1.05$, defines the spatial mean.
Amplitude and residual noise are fitted only on the other three positions.
Gaussian updates combine a healthy hypothesis with a uniform spatial prior;
their conditional-independence assumptions and weights are not field-calibrated.
Adaptive disagreement selects the next path from predicted response variance
under current weights and observes only that selected test path. Cyclic,
geometry-spread, training-static, and seeded random sequences share inference
and reveal budgets. Sixteen random orders per case generate policy variability.
The 960 trajectories reuse measured banks and are not 960 independent
measurements or physical experiments.

At 32 revealed paths with condition-matched references, adaptive conditional
MAP error is 160.76 mm versus 190.70 mm for random order; conditional 50 mm
success is 25.00\% versus 8.85\%. These location scores are computed within the
damage-cell map even when the separate damage decision is negative. Adaptive
damage-positive rate is only 50\%, while the healthy false-positive
condition--model rate is 66.67\%. The latter comprises twelve model evaluations
reusing just three unique healthy test banks across four calibration folds.
Thus, conditional localization improvement does not constitute successful
operational NDE.

Full 66-path inference has mean error 213.28 mm and full-bank classical RAPID
209.43 mm under matched references. Adding paths does not repair the declared
response model. Applying the 20~$^{\circ}$C baseline at all temperatures gives
adaptive error 200.04 mm and no 50 mm successes. Condition matching is not
unseen-temperature generalization. One plate and four surrogate locations
support descriptive model/policy comparison, not a large independent-sample
efficacy claim or validation of the 16 kHz ARGUS probe.

\subsection{Cross-Panel Operator Quality: Negative Transfer Result}
The verified CC-BY-4.0 MORPHO curated record~\cite{stamatelatos2026morphodata}
contains 12,504 native 1 MHz traces from five panels, with 1,011 actively
selected operator labels: 875 GOOD and 136 BAD. These labels concern clean,
noisy, saturated, low-energy, or drifting waveforms. They are not defect
positions or an unbiased acquisition sample. Sixteen waveform-only features
from the first 500 samples feed fixed logistic regression and a 300-tree forest
in five leave-panel-out folds. Training-only scaling, a majority dummy, and
one within-training-panel shuffled-label forest provide controls. All labelled
rows receive out-of-fold predictions.

At the frozen 0.5 threshold, the forest rejects only 1/136 BAD signals despite
ordinary accuracy 0.8595 and AUROC 0.7715; balanced accuracy is 0.4997.
Logistic regression rejects 27/136, with balanced accuracy 0.5124 and AUROC
0.2675. The majority dummy achieves accuracy 0.8655 by rejecting no BAD signals;
the shuffled-training forest rejects none. FOD6 has only GOOD labels, so its
fold AUROC and balanced accuracy remain undefined. Strong panel-dependent
label prevalence and discrepancies between README cycle counts and actual
HDF5 groups remain documented. No threshold was retuned on held-out labels,
and no learned quality classifier was promoted to the live controller.

\subsection{Healthy Reference Transport and Confirmation Gate}
The false alarms motivate a separate, healthy-only development branch using
established baseline stretch and phase-compensation principles
\cite{croxford2010temperature,herdovics2019phase}. Recorded electrical pitch
aligns source-clock delay before a bounded receiver time-scale, residual delay
and gain are fitted to first-cycle healthy anchors at nominal 20, 40 and
60~$^{\circ}$C. Prediction interpolates in actual mean surface temperature;
it cannot fit a warp to a damaged/test catch. Immutable policy, template and
source identities accompany each prediction. This remains an affine
approximation to a dispersive, temperature-dependent wavefield.

The initial $\pm1\%$ stretch domain qualified zero of 66 receiver paths;
that failure and its exact source were archived. Expanding the healthy-development
domain to $\pm5\%$ with a fixed $41\times41$ search retained the same
source-correlation, residual and identifiability limits. Mean healthy
40~$^{\circ}$C leave-temperature-out DI decreased from 0.271 to 0.063.
These diagnostics include \emph{all} 66 paths, including unqualified fits;
only 20 paths met qualification across the anchors. A 16-path horizon was
therefore frozen from healthy evidence. The residual scale is a held-out
healthy-temperature proxy, not an independently estimated noise distribution.

A new, explicitly exploratory reanalysis of the previously seen banks compares
three likelihood variants on this common eligible path set and horizon.
Adaptive conditional MAP errors are 159.45 mm for the raw global-scale model,
171.92 mm for transported references with global scale, and 170.03 mm with
path-dependent residual scales. Thus, improved healthy waveform agreement
did not improve localization in this development experiment. These are
conditional-location diagnostics, not new blind tests or evidence that
transport guarantees better decisions.

An experimental reference gate uses a complete healthy trajectory as one
calibration unit. Each calibration score is the maximum, over the fixed budget
prefixes, of the mean squared normalized selected-path DI. The current observed
prefix score is compared with those complete-trajectory maxima. A rank comparison gives
\begin{equation}
 p_{\rm ref}=\frac{1+\#\{j:A_j\geq A_{\rm current}\}}{n+1},
 \label{eq:measured-reference-rank}
\end{equation}
where each $A_j$ belongs to an entire healthy block. Such ranking principles
are established \cite{shafer2008conformal}; interpreting the complete-prefix
maximum requires frozen control/reference identities and exchangeable healthy
blocks. At least 19 blocks are required for 5\% rank resolution. The initial
development check reused only three old healthy banks and consequently returned
unsupported gate decisions. Paths and Monte Carlo orders were not promoted
into independent calibration blocks; that incomplete development result remains
retained separately from the later confirmation.

The expanded verification suite passes 662 backend/acquisition tests, with one deliberate legacy-pickle skip, and 34 frontend tests, type checking, lint and build. These checks do not establish physical inspection efficacy.

The planned 29-bank confirmation completed on 9 October after temporary
HTTP 403 download failures were resolved through a public retry. All 29
complete native banks, totaling 403,070,304 bytes, passed member-size/CRC and
local SHA-256 verification. They comprise 19 healthy calibration blocks and ten
separate 30/50~$^{\circ}$C confirmation banks. The report filename retains the
8 October protocol tag. Predictions, fitting policy
and controller parameters were frozen before reading confirmation catches;
source hashes remained unchanged during scoring. The report retains 864
trajectories and 216 controller-bound calibrations, each using the same 19
physical blocks, with zero recorded rejections. These counts are not independent
specimens or newly acquired adaptive experiments.

At 16 paths, adaptive conditional MAP errors are 161.20 mm for raw global
scaling, 206.49 mm for transported global scaling, and 215.39 mm for
path-dependent transported scaling. Transport therefore worsened this
confirmation localization metric. Across all three adaptive variants, no
allowed prefix produced a supported anomaly: the two unique healthy test
banks had zero false alarms, but the eight mass-position/temperature conditions
had zero detections. Zero false alarms in this setting reflects an insensitive,
conservative gate and is not successful NDE. The new temperatures reuse the
same plate and four removable-mass positions. Correlated cooling calibration
blocks and unverified exchangeability still prevent a field-coverage claim.

For each transported random-order variant, 10 of 128 damaged-condition order
runs produced an early supported alert, with none in 128 healthy-condition
order runs. Averaging the 16 order repetitions within each of eight mass/temperature
conditions first gives a 7.8125\% mean any-prefix alert frequency. These are
replays of the same measured banks, not ten independent physical detections.
Raw random order and every geometry-spread variant produced no supported alerts.
No variant retained a supported alert at the final budget; the reported
any-prefix counts preserve early events that a final-only summary would miss.

Experimental dispositions request qualified references or diverse verification, with stale
identity/index guards. They authorize neither an actuator nor a healthy
structural release. The ordered clock/reference qualification and model/control
feedback remain candidates for separate prior-art and human-inventorship
review; BSS, phase compensation and conformal ranking are not claimed novel.

Measured-data replay consequently provides inspectable positive and negative
development and confirmation evidence, while apparatus validation and operational NDE remain open.
